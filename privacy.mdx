**Effective October 21, 2026.**

This policy explains what personal information Builddown AI, LLC collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

We collect only what we need to run our services. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Who we are

"BuildDown", "we", and "us" mean Builddown AI, LLC, a Colorado limited liability company. You can reach us at [contact@builddown.ai](mailto:contact@builddown.ai).

## What this policy covers

- The website at builddown.ai and this documentation site.
- The BuildDown control plane at auth.builddown.ai, including "Sign in with BuildDown".
- AI-Implement orchestrators that BuildDown sets up and runs for our customers, when you sign in to them.

Third-party services that we link to have their own policies.

## Information we collect

### When you sign in

You sign in to BuildDown with your Google account. Google gives us:

- your name
- your email address, and whether Google has verified it
- your profile picture
- a unique Google account identifier

We ask Google only for the `openid`, `email`, and `profile` scopes. We do not get access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar, or any other Google data.

### While you use our services

- **Session data:** a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and the IP address and browser type of each session.
- **Access records:** which orchestrators you signed in to, and when.
- **Operational records:** logs that we keep to run, secure, and fix the service.

### On our websites

- If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email address.
- Standard request data, such as IP address and browser type, that our hosting providers process to deliver and protect the sites.

## How we use information

- To sign you in to BuildDown and to the orchestrators you are allowed to use.
- To decide what you can access, based on the access list that your organization sets.
- To keep the services secure, detect abuse, and investigate problems.
- To send the newsletter, if you subscribed.
- To meet legal obligations.

## Google user data

BuildDown's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

We use Google account information only to sign you in and to identify you to the orchestrators you use. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train AI models.

## Who we share information with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run BuildDown, and only as needed:

- **Google**: sign-in.
- **Fly.io**: hosting for the control plane and for orchestrators.
- **Neon**: the database that stores accounts and sessions.
- **Cloudflare**: DNS, and hosting for the builddown.ai website.
- **Mintlify**: hosting for this documentation site.
- **Buttondown**: the newsletter, if you subscribe.

When you sign in to an orchestrator, we send that orchestrator your name and email address so that it can apply your organization's access list.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect the rights and safety of our users or of BuildDown, or as part of a merger or sale of the business. We will tell you about such a change of ownership.

## Customer data in orchestrators

Orchestrators process our customers' code, tickets, and related content for their teams. Our agreement with each customer covers that content.

If your organization uses BuildDown, contact your organization with questions about that content.

## How long we keep information

- **Account information:** while your account is active, and up to 30 days after you ask us to delete it.
- **Sessions:** until they expire or you sign out.
- **Logs:** up to 90 days, unless we need them longer to investigate a security issue.

## How we protect information

We encrypt data in transit and at rest, limit access to the people who need it, keep credentials out of logs, and isolate each customer's orchestrator.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.

## Your choices and rights

- To see, correct, or delete your personal information, email [contact@builddown.ai](mailto:contact@builddown.ai).
- To remove BuildDown's access to your Google account, go to [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
- To stop the newsletter, use the unsubscribe link in any issue.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under local law. We respond to requests within 30 days.

## Children

BuildDown is for businesses. It is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Where we process information

We process and store information in the United States.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we tell signed-in users before it takes effect.
